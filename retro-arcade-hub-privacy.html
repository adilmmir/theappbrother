App privacy

Retro Arcade Hub
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026
Developer: The App Brother (TAB)
Android package: com.theappbrother.retroarcadehub
This notice describes version 0.5.1 and its current offline features.

Your play stays on your device.
  • No sign-in or online accounts.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.
  • No app Internet permission or cloud synchronization.
  • Local player profiles, high scores and settings, with deletion controls.

1. Information stored on your device

You may choose a local player nickname. Retro Arcade Hub stores player nicknames, per-game high scores, the selected player, the visual theme and the touch-feedback setting in Android’s private app storage. These are offline player profiles, not online accounts. You can use a nickname rather than your real name.

2. Collection and sharing

This version does not transmit your player information, gameplay or device information to The App Brother. It has no Internet permission, developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, in-app purchases or cloud synchronization. The app does not sell or share data with third parties.

Android, Google Play and other services you use separately have their own privacy practices. This policy describes the app’s own handling, not every activity performed by your device or app store.

3. Retention and deletion

Your local information remains until you delete it. In Your Arcade → Delete Current Player, confirm deletion to remove the selected player’s saved profile and high scores. Other players and device settings remain. If no players remain, the app creates an empty default “Player 1” profile.

To remove all local player information and settings, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. The App Brother has no server copy to restore or delete. The app is configured to disable Android cloud backup and exclude its app data from device-to-device transfer.

4. Permissions and children’s privacy

This version does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, shared-file or other runtime permissions. It has no social features, messaging, advertising, purchases or external data submissions, and does not ask for a date of birth.

Local nicknames and scores remain on the device as described above. Please do not send children’s real names or unnecessary sensitive information in support messages. A parent or guardian can use the app’s player-deletion controls or Android’s clear-storage controls to remove local information.

5. Security

Android’s private app storage helps isolate local preferences from ordinary access by other apps. The app does not add a separate password lock or encryption layer to player profiles. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android updated. The app is configured not to allow cleartext network traffic; this offline version makes no network requests.

6. If you contact us

Emailing theappbrother@gmail.com sends your email address, message and any attachments through your email provider and Gmail. We use correspondence to read and respond to your request and follow up on the issue you raise. Only include information needed for your request; do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Correspondence may be retained for follow-up and applicable obligations. We do not promise a fixed retention period or deletion deadline. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of correspondence we hold; email providers’ own practices may also apply.

7. Visiting this policy page

This policy page contains no advertising or analytics scripts. Render hosts and delivers the website, so visiting the page sends connection and request information, including your IP address and requested page, to the hosting infrastructure. Those website requests are separate from the offline app. The website’s other pages may use advertising as described in the website privacy policy. See also Render’s privacy information.

8. Changes and contact

If the app adds online services, advertising or other data handling, we will review and update this policy and the app’s privacy disclosures before releasing those changes. The effective date above identifies this notice.
