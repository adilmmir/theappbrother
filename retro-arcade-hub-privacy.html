App privacy

Retro Arcade Hub
Privacy Policy

Updated: 25 November 2026 · The App Brother
Android package: com.theappbrother.retroarcadehub
Version 0.6.0 with optional Google Play purchases.

Your progress stays on your device.

No app sign-in. No ads. Optional Google Play purchase. Games work offline.

1. Local player information

The app stores local nicknames, high scores, campaign levels reached, selected player, theme and touch-feedback setting in Android private app storage. These are offline profiles, not online accounts. Use a nickname rather than a real name. The App Brother does not operate a gameplay/profile server or receive your local nicknames and scores.

2. Google Play purchases

Three games are free. An optional one-time, non-consumable purchase unlocks the other seven for all local players on the device. Google Play handles payment and the purchasing Google account. We do not receive card numbers or payment credentials. Google may process transaction, account, device, network and diagnostic information under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . The app receives product ID, purchase state, token, time, order information when available and a signed receipt. The receipt and signature stay in private storage to verify offline access. Local nicknames are not sent as billing account identifiers. This version has no developer-operated receipt server.

3. Network and SDKs

Version 0.6.0 includes Internet and network-state access for the Google Play Billing Library and supporting components. Billing may connect to Google on app launch or resume to check purchases, even if you do not buy. Games and local progress work offline. Purchases and restoring require a connection. There are no ads, social features or separate gameplay analytics SDKs. Google billing and its supporting components may process diagnostic and connection information to provide and protect the service. Earlier version 0.5.1 has no billing or app Internet permission.

4. Retention and deletion

Your Arcade > Delete Current Player removes that player nickname, scores and campaign progress. Other profiles, settings and the shared purchase remain. An empty Player 1 profile is created if no players remain. Clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove all local profiles, settings and cached receipts. Android cloud backup and app-data transfer are disabled. No developer server copy of progress exists. Deleting a player or uninstalling does not refund/cancel a Google Play purchase or delete Google transaction records. Restore Purchases online with the same purchasing Google account restores the unlock, but not local game progress. Google Play manages refunds and transaction records under its policies.

5. Children and permissions

The app has no chat, public profiles, ads, or camera, microphone, contacts, location or shared-file requests. It offers a purchase through Google Play; parents can use Play purchase authentication and family controls to manage spending. It does not request a birth date. Avoid real names or sensitive information in local nicknames and support messages. A parent or guardian may use the deletion controls.

6. Security

Android private storage isolates local preferences from ordinary access by other apps. There is no separate profile password lock. Purchase signatures are checked before access is granted; pending/unverified payments do not unlock games. This version verifies receipts on the device rather than a developer server. Device-side checks cannot eliminate tampering. Offline cached access may only reflect cancellation/revocation when Google Play next successfully checks purchases. Cleartext network traffic is disabled. Protect your device with a screen lock and updates.

7. Support and contact

Email theappbrother@gmail.com for privacy questions. Emailing sends your address, message and attachments through your email provider and Gmail. We use correspondence to respond and follow up. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive information. Correspondence may be retained for follow-up and applicable obligations; contact us about access, correction or deletion. Providers may have their own retention practices.

8. Website and changes

This policy page has no advertising or analytics scripts. Render hosts the website and receives connection/request information, including IP address and requested page. Website requests are separate from local progress and billing. Other website pages may use advertising as described in the website privacy policy. See https://render.com/privacy and https://www.theappbrother.com/privacy.html#website . We review this policy when features or practices change.
