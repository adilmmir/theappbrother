Scope

This page contains separate privacy policies for the TAB studio website and the Dreamwiser Android app. TAB — The App Brother is the app developer. The app policy below describes version 0.1.0, including its local journal, advertising services and current limitation for distribution to children.

Website privacy policy

This section describes the static studio website hosted at theappbrother.onrender.com, based on its current published source.

Website features and browser storage

The website provides information about TAB and its apps. Its current source contains no account registration, contact submission form, advertising SDK, analytics integration, or code that sets cookies or uses browser local storage. Styles and the favicon are served with the site; its only homepage script displays the current year. These statements concern the website code, not Dreamwiser or infrastructure-level processing.

Hosting and technical requests

Render hosts and delivers the website. Visiting it sends connection and request information to the hosting infrastructure, including your IP address and the page requested. Infrastructure may process additional request metadata or logs to deliver and protect the service. TAB has not yet verified the exact logged fields, access controls, processing locations, or retention periods applicable to this site. See Render’s privacy information for its own practices. This notice does not promise that visiting the site involves no data processing.

Contacting TAB

You can email theappbrother@gmail.com with privacy or support questions. Emailing us sends your email address, message and any attachments through your email service and Gmail. Only include information needed for your request; please avoid sending private dream descriptions or other sensitive material unnecessarily.

Contact information is used to read and respond to your request and follow up on the issue you raise. The static website itself does not receive or store your email message. A fixed retention period, mailbox access/security procedures, and any exceptional retention grounds for TAB’s support correspondence remain to be confirmed.

Retention, security and deletion

The site is available over HTTPS, which protects the connection to the site. This is not a claim about encryption or security inside Dreamwiser. Hosting-log retention and TAB’s correspondence retention schedule remain unverified. To ask about access, correction or deletion of correspondence held by TAB, email the privacy contact below; identify the relevant correspondence without sending passwords. No automated deletion service or completion deadline is represented here. Hosting and email providers’ processing and retention may also apply.

Other websites and changes

Following an external link or using email involves the destination provider’s own practices. This notice will be revised as the website or verified handling practices change; the date above identifies this version.

Dreamwiser privacy policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026. This policy covers Dreamwiser, developed by TAB — The App Brother, Android package com.theappbrother.dreamwisdom. The current version 0.1.0 displays the name “DreamWisdom” inside the app.

What the app does

Dreamwiser lets you enter a dream, find matching symbols in a bundled dream dictionary, read alternative interpretations and save a personal guest journal. Dream matching and dictionary searches run on your device. Dream text is not sent to an online AI service. The current app has no sign-in, user accounts, developer-operated backend or cloud synchronization.

Your dreams and local storage

Text you enter is processed locally to find dictionary entries. If you choose “Save to my journal,” the app stores your dream text, matching symbol references, a locally generated record ID and the save date in its private WebView storage on your device. It also remembers whether you completed onboarding. Unsaved dream text is held in the current app session rather than added to your journal.

TAB does not receive or upload your dream text, saved journal or dictionary search terms. These are not included in advertising requests. A local counter records completed dream submissions to limit full-screen ad frequency; the counter contains no dream text. Google’s consent tools also maintain advertising privacy choices and related state.

Advertising and information sent off the device

The Android app includes Google AdMob banner and full-screen advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising privacy choices. Internet access is used by these services even though the dream dictionary and journal work locally. The browser preview shows advertising placeholders rather than live AdMob ads.

Google’s advertising services can collect and share your device’s IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions, technical performance and diagnostic information, and identifiers such as an advertising ID or app set ID, where available and permitted. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, support advertising analytics and prevent fraud. The information processed depends on your device, applicable privacy choices and Google’s service configuration. Google also processes information needed to determine and remember applicable advertising choices. Dream content is not provided to these services by the app.

Advertising data is processed by Google and applicable advertising partners under their own practices. See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google’s advertising SDK data disclosures. TAB has not added a separate analytics or crash-reporting service to this version; the advertising SDK’s own diagnostics still apply.

Advertising privacy choices

The app requests updated consent information and displays a Google privacy form when Google’s configured messages require one. Ad loading is gated by UMP’s permission-to-request-ads status. In Settings, select “Manage advertising privacy” to reopen available privacy options. If Google reports that no additional options are required, the app displays a message explaining that. Available forms depend on your region and the messages configured for the app. These forms do not constitute parental consent.

You can also use Android’s advertising privacy settings to reset or delete your advertising ID where supported. Changing advertising choices does not delete your local journal or necessarily stop all processing needed for permitted ads, security or consent management.

Permissions and device access

The app uses internet access and network-state access for advertising and consent services. Its release package also declares advertising ID and Android advertising-services permissions for identifiers, attribution and topics, plus wake-lock and foreground-service permissions added by its dependencies. Permission availability does not mean every associated feature is used on every device.

This version does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS-reading, call-log, precise-location or shared photo/file-storage permissions. The journal uses the app’s private storage without requesting access to your shared files. Approximate location may still be inferred from the IP address received by Google; it is not obtained through a device location permission.

Security

Journal data is stored within the app’s private storage. Android backup is disabled in the app configuration. The app does not add its own password lock or separate encryption layer to journal records; device security therefore matters. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android updated.

The app disables unencrypted HTTP traffic, restricts its WebView to packaged content and disables direct file/content access. The connection between the packaged page and native advertising controls is restricted to the app’s local content origin. Google documents encryption in transit for advertising SDK data. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Saved dreams remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. In “My journal,” choose “Delete” and confirm to remove an individual record. To remove the whole journal, open Settings, choose “Delete all saved dreams” and confirm. Clearing app storage through Android settings also removes local preferences and advertising-frequency state. TAB has no server copy of your journal to restore or delete. The browser preview can export journal files; copies you export must be deleted separately.

Removing the app or deleting journal records does not erase information previously processed by Google. Google’s retention and deletion practices are described in its privacy policy and privacy controls. If you email TAB, your email address, message and any attachments are handled through email providers including Gmail to respond to your request. Correspondence may be retained for follow-up and applicable obligations; we do not promise a fixed deletion deadline. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of correspondence we hold.

Children and parents

The intended audience includes children under 13. Parents and guardians should review this policy and help children avoid putting names, contact details or other sensitive information into dreams or support messages. Children’s dream entries follow the same local-storage and deletion behavior described above; TAB does not receive their journal through the app.

Current-build limitation: the advertising integration in version 0.1.0 does not yet implement an age screen or child-directed advertising configuration. The advertising data practices above must not be read as a promise of child-specific protections. This build is not ready for distribution to children. Child-appropriate advertising and identifier restrictions, the audience configuration and consent requirements must be implemented and verified before such distribution. A privacy form or this policy alone does not provide those safeguards.

A parent or guardian can delete journal entries on the child’s device using the controls above and contact TAB about any information sent to us by email. Please do not email a child’s journal or unnecessary identifying information.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change and revise the effective date above. For privacy questions or requests, email theappbrother@gmail.com, with “Dreamwiser privacy” in the subject.
