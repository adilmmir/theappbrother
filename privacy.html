Scope

Website privacy policy

This section describes the static studio website hosted at theappbrother.onrender.com, based on its current published source.

Website features and browser storage

The website provides information about TAB and its apps. Its current source contains no account registration, contact submission form, advertising SDK, analytics integration, or code that sets cookies or uses browser local storage. Styles and the favicon are served with the site; its only homepage script displays the current year. These statements concern the website code, not Dreamwiser or infrastructure-level processing.

Hosting and technical requests

Render hosts and delivers the website. Visiting it sends connection and request information to the hosting infrastructure, including your IP address and the page requested. Infrastructure may process additional request metadata or logs to deliver and protect the service. TAB has not yet verified the exact logged fields, access controls, processing locations, or retention periods applicable to this site. See Render’s privacy information for its own practices. This notice does not promise that visiting the site involves no data processing.

Contacting TAB

You can email theappbrother@gmail.com with privacy or support questions. Emailing us sends your email address, message and any attachments through your email service and Gmail. Only include information needed for your request; please avoid sending private dream descriptions or other sensitive material unnecessarily.

Contact information is used to read and respond to your request and follow up on the issue you raise. The static website itself does not receive or store your email message. A fixed retention period, mailbox access/security procedures, and any exceptional retention grounds for TAB’s support correspondence remain to be confirmed.

Retention, security and deletion

The site is available over HTTPS, which protects the connection to the site. This is not a claim about encryption or security inside Dreamwiser. Hosting-log retention and TAB’s correspondence retention schedule remain unverified. To ask about access, correction or deletion of correspondence held by TAB, email the privacy contact below; identify the relevant correspondence without sending passwords. No automated deletion service or completion deadline is represented here. Hosting and email providers’ processing and retention may also apply.

Other websites and changes

Following an external link or using email involves the destination provider’s own practices. This notice will be revised as the website or verified handling practices change; the date above identifies this version.

Dreamwiser — app-specific disclosures pending

Not ready for Google Play submission. Dreamwiser’s app name has been confirmed, but its Android source, package ID, release configuration and Google Play listing have not been verified. This section is a disclosure status notice, not a completed privacy policy for the app. Do not interpret missing information as “no data collected,” “no sharing,” or “no SDKs.”

The following facts must be established for the actual release before an app-specific policy can be finalized:

  • App identity: package ID, release/version and exact Play developer identity.
  • Data access and collection: data processed only on-device versus sent off-device; whether dream text, journal entries, identifiers, diagnostics, account information or other personal/sensitive data are accessed; purposes and whether each use is required or optional. These are questions, not claims that Dreamwiser processes these data.
  • Permissions and services: Android permissions and features; all libraries/SDKs, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud or AI services and their enabled configurations; recipients and onward sharing. None are currently verified.
  • Security: actual transport protection for every app and SDK data flow, storage protection and access procedures.
  • Retention and deletion: retention periods or criteria by data type, local-data controls, backend/backup deletion and any justified retention exceptions; whether accounts can be created and the working in-app and web account-deletion paths if required.
  • Audience: intended ages and whether children are included, with any applicable child-directed collection, advertising and consent arrangements.

Privacy questions about Dreamwiser can be sent to theappbrother@gmail.com. This contact link does not claim that an account-deletion system has been implemented. Once the missing facts are verified, this section will link to a completed Dreamwiser policy.
